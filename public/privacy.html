Privacy

Leave Ledger early preview · Updated October 29, 2026

Leave Ledger is in early preview. It is for trying the product with made-up company and employee details. Please do not enter real employee names, dates or health information.

When you sign in with Google

  • We receive your name, email address, your Google account ID, and your company domain if you use a Google Workspace account.
  • We only ask Google for basic profile and email. We do not get access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar or contacts, and we never see your password.

What else we keep

  • What you type into the app: company settings, made-up employees, leave requests and notes.
  • Feedback you send and your survey answers.
  • Simple activity counts, such as when you last used the app and how many changes you made, so we can see what gets used.

Clerk, the built-in assistant

Text you type for Clerk is sent to Anthropic, which runs the AI model, so it can fill in the request form. Clerk only suggests; a user always confirms before anything is saved.

Who can see it

  • Inside a workspace, each user sees what their role allows. HR admins see everything, managers see dates and coverage for their team, and employees see their own leave.
  • The team building Leave Ledger can see workspace names, user emails, activity counts, feedback and survey answers.
  • We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

The app and its data are hosted by Netlify. Fonts load from Google Fonts. We use sign-in cookies only, with no advertising or tracking cookies.

Deleting your information

To delete your account, a workspace, or your feedback and answers, contact the team that invited you.
